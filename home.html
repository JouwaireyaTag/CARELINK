How CARELINK Works

1
Create Your Profile

Patients document their allergies, medications, and past surgeries in a structured digital profile.

2
Approve & Share OTP

During a clinic visit, the doctor sends an access request and you authorize them with a 6-digit OTP.

3
Update & Track Care

The doctor reviews your history safely and logs new consultation notes directly into your record.

Designed with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), expiring permissions, and comprehensive audit trails to keep sensitive patient records protected at all times.
